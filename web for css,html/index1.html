MOUNTAINS

Ella

Explore green hills, scenic trails and the famous Nine Arch Bridge.

View Details

Ella is surrounded by green mountains, tea plantations and waterfalls.

Places to visit

  • Nine Arch Bridge
  • Little Adam’s Peak
  • Ella Rock
  • Ravana Falls

Things to do

Enjoy hiking, photography, scenic train journeys and visits to tea plantations.

BEACHES

Mirissa

Enjoy ocean views, sandy beaches and peaceful sunsets.

View Details

Mirissa is a coastal destination in southern Sri Lanka, known for its beaches and ocean views.

Places to visit

  • Mirissa Beach
  • Coconut Tree Hill
  • Secret Beach

Things to do

Enjoy beach walks, sunset photography and local seafood.

HERITAGE

Sigiriya

Discover ancient history, landscaped gardens and incredible views.

View Details

Sigiriya is an ancient rock fortress with landscaped gardens, historic paintings and panoramic views.

Places to visit

  • Sigiriya Rock Fortress
  • Sigiriya Museum
  • Water Gardens
  • Nearby Pidurangala Rock

Things to do

Explore ancient ruins, discover Sri Lankan history and enjoy landscape photography.
